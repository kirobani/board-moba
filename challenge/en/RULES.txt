BOARD MOBA — COMPLETE PLAYING RULES

Both teams see the current board. Hidden information includes the actual seed, unannounced ancient objectives, and enemy orders for the current turn. Each side may submit one order per hero per map turn. BALANCE.md and config.json give the active numeric parameters. The provided engine governs exact resolution.

1. Board, fountain and movement
There are 39 hexes, three lanes, two tower tiers per lane, six ordinary camps per side and two ancient sites. Team A's base is (-3,3) and fountain (-4,4); B's base is (3,-3) and fountain (4,-4). Each fountain connects only to its own base. Heroes can share a tile, pass through one another or swap positions, and enter either base or fountain. Fountains never attack and heal their own team only. Heroes spawn, respawn or successfully recall in their own fountain.
Move travels to one neighboring tile and consumes the turn's action. Corresponding camps and towers mirror via (q,r) -> (r,q) within the same lane. Both ancient sites are on the mirror axis.

2. Roles and starting stats
Top: 400 HP, 70 attack, teleport from turn 31, cooldown 20.
Mid: 400 HP, 70 attack, can cast a three-tile spell or hit all enemies in the same engagement, cooldown 2.
ADC: 320 HP, 105 attack, +2 gold per eligible lane pool. Reap deals 1.2 times attack rounded down, map range 2, cooldown 5 or death refresh.
Support: 400 HP, 70 healing, can heal, control, engage, push and capture ancients. Cannot capture ordinary camps or attack heroes on the map.
Jungle: 400 HP, 70 attack, earns 1.5 times normal camp gold and counts double when capturing ancients.

3. Map orders
Use one order from move, attack, spell, reap, capture, push, heal, control, recall, wait, engage and teleport. A missing or duplicate order becomes wait. An illegal order does nothing. Dead and controlled heroes cannot take an active action. See API.txt for JSON forms.
Attack: Top, mid, ADC and jungle target one living enemy at distance 0–1. No cooldown. All lane tiles, towers, bases and fountains count as lane terrain. The 14 resource tiles are jungle. Attack and reap from a jungle tile can target only jungle enemies, unless both are in the same engagement. A target need not be on the same named lane.
Spell: Mid selects three different connected tiles, all within a distance of 1 of the caster. Deal current attack once to each living enemy on the selected tiles, crossing lane/jungle boundaries. No damage to allies, resources or buildings. In an engagement, omitting cells hits every living enemy in the same engagement once, without range or terrain limits. Both modes cost one action and share cooldown: used on R, next ready on R+2. A valid spell into empty tiles consumes cooldown. An invalid selection does not.
Reap: ADC targets one living enemy at distance 0–2, or any living enemy in the same engagement. Deal floor(1.2 × attack). Used on R, next ready on R+5. Refresh only if that enemy was actually hit by that ADC's reap and dies in the same map turn. A refresh gives no extra action. A target moving outside a previously legal range may dodge; cooldown is consumed either way.
Capture: Target a living ordinary camp or ancient objective on your tile. Support can capture only ancients. Capture has no cooldown and deals no damage to towers or bases.
Push: Name top, mid or bottom. Stand on the rear tile of that lane's shared income zone, toward your own base. Any role can push any eligible lane, including in the first one-tile mid zone. If multiple zones intersect at a base, choose one lane. One contribution per hero per turn, independent of other cooldowns.
Heal: Support targets one living ally within distance 1, including itself, or any living ally in the same engagement. Heal for current healing, capped at maximum HP. Terrain does not restrict healing. No cooldown; healing a full ally does not consume control cooldown.
Control: Support targets a living enemy at distance 0–1, or an enemy anywhere in the same engagement. A legal target is guaranteed to lose its next action and any pending recall is interrupted. Cooldown 3 (R+3 is ready); `utilityReady` tracks control.
Recall: Channel for one turn and return to your fountain at full HP. Nearby enemies do not prevent attempting a recall. Damage, control, death or a new engagement can interrupt it. A hero already controlled or locked in an engagement cannot recall.
Wait: Stay in place and take automatic income if eligible. Engage and teleport are described below.

4. Gold and stats
Initial gold is 0. Gold increases base attack/healing and maximum HP automatically. There are different rates for the first 300 gold and any excess. Use the active rates in BALANCE.md and growthCurve in config.json. Each added percentage is applied to the starting stat, rounded down. The engine uses the start-of-turn stats for the current turn's actions. Gold updates stats at the end of the turn and preserves the hero's current HP percentage. A fountain can then restore full HP.

5. Lane income
Each team has its own independent 12-gold pool per lane per turn. Both teams share the same physical tiles. Top and bottom start with the two tiles between their outer towers: top (-2,-1), (-1,-2) and bottom (1,2), (2,1). Mid starts at (0,0). Pushes change zones. Enemy movement alone does not. Fountains are outside the zones.
Turns 1–40: top can earn only in top lane, mid only in mid, ADC and support only in bottom, jungle cannot collect lane income. From turn 41 any role can earn in any lane. Use living status and positions after movement, battle and successful recall, before pushes. A hero shoved back by a push still gets income based on its previous position. Each pool covering a tile pays independently, including overlapping lanes at a base.
Divide each pool by eligible weights, with denominator at least 1. Most roles weigh 1 and support weighs 0.25. ADC gets a flat +2 per eligible pool. Support receives 50% extra on its share when its ADC qualifies in the same pool. Examples for one pool: solo regular 12, solo ADC 14, ADC plus support 11.6 and 3.6, ADC plus regular 8 and 6, solo support 3. If all three pools genuinely cover the same tile, all three can pay; solo regular 36, solo ADC 42, solo support 9, ADC and support 34.8 and 10.8.

5a. Lane push
Compare the number of valid pushers still living after combat. On a tie, compare their summed start-of-turn attack, using heal power for support. If both tie, or no one pushes, the lane does not move. New control blocks the next turn's push, while existing control blocks this turn.
The first successful mid push expands the one-tile zone to a second tile containing the enemy outer tower, with no damage or movement. Later pushes can shift the two-tile zone in either direction.
If the forward tile has no living enemy structure, shift the zone one tile toward the enemy base, advance winning pushers one tile, and shove all living enemies in the old zone one tile toward their base. Non-pushing allies do not advance. A locked engagement hero is immune to pushback. Each hero moves at most once after resolving all lanes on the same pre-push snapshot. Overlaps prioritize that hero's own push advance, then retreat along the assigned lane, then top, mid, bottom. Update engagement memberships after the movement.
If the forward tile contains a damageable enemy structure, the winning pushers damage it and no one or zone moves. Damage per pusher, including support, is min(4, 1 + floor(start-of-turn gold / 500)). The turn 1–40 team caps are in BALANCE.md. An ally structure or ruin does not block a push. After destruction, advance is possible from the next turn. A tower or base can be entered freely but can only be damaged by a valid push. Attacks and capture do not damage structures. Protection is checked at the start of the turn, so destroying one structure cannot immediately unlock its successor that same turn.

6. Ordinary camps and ancients
A live camp has one durability. Capture it from its tile in one turn. Solo normal role gets 32 gold, jungle gets 48. If contested, count valid capturers from both sides. Greater count wins; ties are random at 50%. A camp taken on R is available again on R+15. Support cannot capture a camp.
The two ancient sites have independent random timers. First spawn is on turn 20–70 inclusive at each site, with a newly drawn delay of 20–70 turns from each capture. A notice 5–20 turns before spawning gives site, exact turn, 3–6 integer durability and 500–1000 integer team gold. The draws are independent and both sites may spawn together. A live ancient persists until captured, without restoring durability or accumulating new spawns. No spawn/notice beyond the map phase.
Same-tile capturers count 1 each except jungle counts 2. At least two total capture points across both sides removes one durability; only one durability can be removed per turn. Capture points reset every turn. On the last point, the team with more points that turn wins the objective, with 50% random tie-break. Earlier contributions do not decide ownership. All five winning heroes are paid, including dead allies: 4/17 each for the four combat roles and 1/17 for support, rounded to millionths. Resources do not attack.

7. Structures
Each lane's outer and inner tower HP is randomly drawn at game start in ranges 30–60 and 40–60 respectively. Opposite towers of the same lane and tier get identical initial HP. HP is public and does not reset. Tower trophies are team pools of 100 outer and 200 inner, awarded once, including to dead teammates, with support weighing 0.25 and other roles 1. The same lane's outer tower must be gone to damage an inner tower. The base has 60 HP, pays no gold and is invulnerable until at least one lane is cleared at the start of the turn. After that it uses ordinary per-hero push damage; the turn 1–40 balance caps apply. Buildings never attack, but early tower cover can reduce hero damage as described in BALANCE.md.

8. Fountain, recall and respawn
A living hero at its own fountain at the end of a map turn returns to full HP, even if it walked there or enemies are nearby. A base or enemy fountain does not heal it. Recall and respawn arrive in the allied fountain.
Respawn delay in full turns for deaths on turns 1–60, 61–120, 121–180, 181–240, 241–300 and 301–360: 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28, respectively. On death turn R with delay D the hero can act on R+D+1.

9. Turn sequence, combat and victory
Prepare respawns, notices and spawns; get both teams' orders; apply engagement and action restrictions; move and teleport; process healing and control intent; register pushes and captures; apply attacks and deaths, then resource rewards; resolve recalls; capture the lane income snapshot; apply pushes and tower rewards; schedule control; pay lane gold, update stats, heal in fountain and check victory.
Validate combat range and targets after movement, with the dodge exception for movement out of legal range. Movement within the selected spell tiles does not trigger dodge. Movement into the area is hit normally. An engagement-wide spell hits each living enemy in the same engagement.
Attack, spell and reap resolve simultaneously on the map: use start-of-turn attack and post-move, post-heal living status. Record all valid hits, subtract HP together and resolve deaths. Already committed lethal attacks can trade kills. Registered resource captures still count; a pusher must survive combat.
A kill pays up to 120. The credited killer gets 80. Give 40 to other allies within one hex of the dead hero according to ordinary weight 1 and support weight 0.25, with no double payment to the killer. With no eligible ally, the 40 goes unpaid. A lone eligible support gets all 40. Use post-move position and heroes alive before combat; allies killed in the same combat remain eligible. Damage is unnecessary for assist eligibility. Credit the attacker that dealt the highest single hit this turn, with hero ID as tiebreaker. Allocate each victim separately. Round eligible assist fractions down to millionths and distribute leftover units in hero ID order.
A base at zero HP loses; simultaneous destruction of both bases is a draw. If neither has fallen at the end of map turn 360, preserve the map state in the replay and enter the final battle below.

10. Final battle
All ten heroes return alive at their current maximum HP. Clear map control, attack and control cooldowns. Gold and stats freeze. No income, new rewards, resources, structure actions, recall or respawn. Map position is irrelevant to actions.
Shuffle the ten IDs uniformly once, publish that order and repeat it cyclically. Ask only the team of the current actor to return that hero's order. Resolve it immediately. Permanently dead heroes are skipped.
Top, mid, ADC and jungle may attack any living enemy each action. Mid can cast its spell without target, to or cells to damage all living enemies. A spell used on personal action N is ready on N+2. ADC reap hits any living enemy for floor(1.2 × attack), ready on N+5, or immediately refreshed if a reap-damaged enemy dies in the same `battle.round`. Clear reap hit records at the start of each cycle. There is no movement dodge.
Support can heal any living ally, including itself, with no cooldown, or control any living enemy. Control skips that enemy's next action, never stacks additional skips, and is ready on the support's N+3. A skipped action still advances personal cooldown time. When support is its team's only surviving hero, it uses its current healing stat as attack and may only attack or wait, even if the enemy still has other roles.
Eliminating all enemies wins. Damage can kill multiple targets in one spell. K/D records still update but give no gold. Both algorithms choose their own targets. A non-terminating healing/control loop can be reported as an incomplete game by the test runner; it is never awarded based on economy or retried according to a preferred outcome.

11. Observation and order protocol
Use order forms in API.txt. `phase` is map, final_pending or final_battle. `mapTurn` is capped at 360, while the replay step `turn` can exceed 360. final_pending is a transition with no decision. `finalDecision` includes actor, team, personalTurn, round, forcedWait, supportSolo, legalActions, controlReadyAt, spellReadyAt and reapReadyAt. aoeReadyAt is a spellReadyAt alias. `battle` exposes order, actor, per-hero personalTurns, spellReady, reapReady, controlReady and skip. In the final battle compare readiness with personalTurn. In the map use decisionTurn, spellTargets, spellEngagementTargets and reapTargets. Your six modules must handle both phases. See CHALLENGE.md for core and coach ordering.

12. Engagement
Jungle and support each unlock engage on turn 21 with separate cooldowns. Coach can use an independent coach_engage on behalf of one hero. Engage chooses a valid center within one hex of the initiator. The center must have at least one living enemy before movement, after respawn. A center with an adjacent enemy but no enemy on it is invalid. The zone is the center and its neighbors; it must contain living heroes from both teams. Failure consumes the order but no cooldown.
Engagements start before movement, recall, capture and push. Living heroes in the region are immediately locked where they stand and their orders for those restricted actions are canceled. The initiator spends the action opening the engagement. Map turns continue with both sides submitting simultaneously.
Participants can wait, attack, spell, reap, heal or control when role and cooldown permit. Against targets in the same engagement, attack, heal and control have no range/terrain limits; mid can use its full-engagement spell; ADC can reap any one enemy. Manual three-tile spell and rules for hitting outside the zone still apply. Outsiders can aid from legal range or move into the zone and join it. A successful outside pusher may enter by advancing; locked heroes cannot be shoved back.
New engagements on the same turn merge only when they share a tile, not merely an edge. Unique living heroes across merged regions set the initial participant count. Disjoint engagements resolve separately. A new overlapping engagement later merges with the ongoing engagement and keeps the old end turn. Every successful initiator is tracked.
The engagement ends as soon as only one side has living participants or the number of elapsed turns reaches the initial participant count. Counting starts on the turn after opening. A turn-21 engagement with six initial heroes ends no later than turn 27. Later joins do not add time. Map economy and respawn continue. A respawned hero in a fountain outside the zone is no longer locked. Turn 360 still ends the map phase.
On the following cleanup turn, former participants generally can only wait or recall. A ready jungle/support on the opposite side of the original initiation may counter-engage. Damage, control or a new engagement can interrupt recalls. Starting from cleanup, each successful initiator begins its own ten-turn cooldown, even if it died or respawned. If the engagement ends on 27, cooldown occupies 28–37 and the skill is ready on 38. Only those who used it go on cooldown. All ordinary map growth and objective mechanics continue in engagements.

13. Top teleport
Top may teleport beginning on turn 31 to the allied base or any original allied tower tile, standing or destroyed. Fountains and enemy structures are illegal. It uses R's order, arrives during normal movement, does not heal, and is ready again on R+20. A canceled or illegal teleport does not consume cooldown. It is unavailable to an engaged or cleanup-locked hero, including a hero caught by an engagement on that same turn. Teleporting in from outside can join an engagement and does not extend it. Normal lane income restrictions still apply.

14. Randomness and economic dodge
Reproducible with the full seed and the same code/orders. Randomness affects ancient schedules, seeded structure HP, resource ties, economic dodge and initial final battle order. Map attacks resolve simultaneously. Unannounced ancient fields are spawnAt:null, maxHP:0, goldPool:null and announcement:null. Notices come before decisions, possibly on turn 0, and do not reroll.
For an otherwise valid attack or reap whose living target was in range before moving and chooses a regular move outside it, dodge probability is clamp(0.50 + 0.01 × (target gold - attacker gold)/100, 0.35, 0.65), using start-of-turn gold. As an example a 50-gold difference changes odds by 0.5 percentage points. Three-tile spells check leaving their selected tiles. A successful dodge prevents the hit; a failed dodge takes full damage. Either consumes the attacker's action and any skill cooldown. The target's move still completes. Control, healing, teleport, illegal attacks and movement that stays in range do not trigger dodge. The whole-engagement spell hits the valid participants directly.

15. Runtime and submission
The six modules share 2,000 ms per synchronous team decision. Total UTF-8 source is capped at 1,048,576 bytes. Each team gets isolated execution per game, with 1,000 ms each for engine startup and top-level module initialization. The development clock starts when the actual task starts, not when queued. Continuations preserve the clock and workspace. Development runs for 180 minutes and the hard submission deadline is 210 minutes, plus any confirmed credited interruption. See CHALLENGE.md and REFEREE.md for submission and grading.
