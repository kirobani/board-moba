# Balance parameters

Gold earned up to 300 and gold earned above 300 use two additive rates based on the starting stat.

| Stat | Per 100 gold up to 300 | Per 100 gold above 300 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Attack and healing | 50% | 25% |
| Non-ADC maximum HP | 40% | 15% |
| ADC maximum HP | 30% | 10% |

At 400 gold an ADC has 288 attack and 640 HP. At 800 gold that is 393 attack and 768 HP.

Through turn 40, building damage from a lane push is capped at 2 total. If the attacking team has no assigned living laner in the income zone of either of the other two lanes, the cap is 1. Eligibility uses pre-push positions. Jungle has no assigned income lane. These caps are lifted from turn 41.

Through turn 40, a hero within one tile of a standing allied tower takes 25% less damage from a hero per hit if at least four enemies are within two tiles and enemies outnumber allies there by at least two. Positions are sampled after movement, before attacks. Map damage resolves simultaneously.
